About

A developer who thinks like a designer.

I'm Abdallah. I design the thing, then I build it — which means the layout you approve is the layout that ships, pixel for pixel.

Most people pick a side. I never could. Design taught me what makes someone stop scrolling; code taught me what it actually costs to build. Sitting in both chairs means I catch the problems early — the typography that breaks on a small screen, the animation that would cost half a second of load time, the brand colour that fails contrast on white.

I work with small businesses, startups and creators who need one person to take an idea from a blank page to something live. No handoff gap, no watered-down build, no waiting on a second freelancer to reply.

  • WordPress & CMS builds
